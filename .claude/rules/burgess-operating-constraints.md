# Burgess Operating Constraints

Always apply these constraints.

- Do not create product features during Phase -1.
- Treat client data, documents, communications and financial records as sensitive.
- Require owner/principal approval before invoices, statements, legal/status communication, marketing or outreach are sent.
- Keep OpenClaw/AI agents draft-only unless a future approved phase explicitly changes the permission model.
- Keep Lexpro as source of truth for legal/trust accounting, bookkeeping, reconciled payments and compliance records.
- Keep Burgess platform as source of truth for invoices and client-facing statement PDFs only.
- Keep financial rules configurable.
- Do not hardcode fallback financial data.
- Use deterministic validation only.
- Do not call AI models from hooks.
